&#x20;                                                                             **Agile vs Waterfall**



Comparison



Point	                                              Agile	                                                                           Waterfall



Planning	The team plans smaller parts of the playlist feature and can change plans based on feedback.	The team generally defines detailed requirements before development starts.



Development	The feature can be developed and released in smaller iterations.	                        Development usually follows the completed requirements and design plan.



Feedback	User and team feedback can be incorporated during development.	                                Feedback is typically incorporated later, especially after planned stages.



Changes	        Changes are easier to accommodate during development.	                                        Changes can be more difficult or expensive after earlier stages are completed.
