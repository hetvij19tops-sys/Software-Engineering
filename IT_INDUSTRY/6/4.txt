Swiggy — Split Bill Feature

1. Requirement

Swiggy identifies the need for a feature that allows friends to split a food order's bill between multiple people.

2. Design

Designers create the split-bill screen showing the total amount, participants and each person's share.

3. Development

Developers write the code required to calculate and process each person's share of the bill.

4. Testing

Testers check whether the bill is divided correctly and test different numbers of people and payment situations.

5. Deployment

Swiggy releases the split-bill feature to users through an app update or controlled rollout.

6. Maintenance

The team fixes bugs and improves the feature after receiving feedback from users.
