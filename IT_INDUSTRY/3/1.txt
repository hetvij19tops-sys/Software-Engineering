**What does each layer do?**

1\. UI — User Interface

UI Layer: This is the visible part of the app that users interact with. It includes restaurant listings, menus, search, cart, payment and order-tracking screens.



2\. Business Logic

Business Logic Layer: It processes user actions and applies the rules of the application, such as calculating prices, placing orders, processing payments and updating delivery status.



3\. Database

Database Layer: It stores important information such as user accounts, restaurant details, menus, orders, payments and reviews.



4\. AI / Model Layer

AI/Model Layer: It uses data and machine-learning models to provide features such as restaurant recommendations, personalized offers and delivery-time predictions.
