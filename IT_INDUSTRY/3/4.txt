**What Happens When You Search for a Song on Spotify?**



* Step 1 — UI

  * type: Dooron Dooron
  * into the search box and press Search.



The mobile app receives the song name that I typed and starts the search request.



* Step 2 — Business Logic



The business logic processes the search request and sends the appropriate request to Spotify's servers



* Step 3 — Request travels to the server

&#x20; Your phone

&#x20;     ↓

&#x20;  Internet

&#x20;     ↓

&#x20;Spotify servers



The server receives your search request.



* Step 4 — Database/Search System



Spotify's backend searches its music catalog for matches.



* Step 5 — AI / Recommendation Systems



Spotify can use information about your listening behavior to personalize what music is recommended to you.

A basic song search does not necessarily mean an AI model is deciding which song exists in the database.

The search itself is primarily a search/retrieval operation; AI can be involved in personalization and ranking around the experience.



* Step 6 — Results come back



Spotify sends the search results back to your phone.
