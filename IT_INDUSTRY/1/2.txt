Product Company : Google

AI work happens inside their core products to make them smarter.
 
Google Maps:
	AI predicts traffic, suggests fastest routes.
Google Translate:
	AI translates text, speech, and even real-time conversations. 	
Google Photos:
	AI automatically recognizes faces, objects, and locations in your photos to organize and let you search them.
