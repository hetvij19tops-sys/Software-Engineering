Daily Task: Sending a Message on WhatsApp

Algorithm
Start.
Open the WhatsApp app.
Select the contact you want to message.
Tap the message box.
Type your message.
Tap the Send button.
Stop.


Step That Could Be Misunderstood

Unclear: "Select the contact."

Clear Version: "Tap the name of the person you want to send the message to from your chat list or contacts."
