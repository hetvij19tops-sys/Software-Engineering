1.Start.
2.Turn on your phone.
3.Unlock your phone.
4.Open the Instagram app.
5.Log in if needed.
6.Tap the + (Create) button, usually at the bottom or top of the screen.
7.Choose Post if Instagram asks what you want to create.
8.A list of photos from your phone will appear.
9.Tap the photo you want to upload.
10.Tap Next.
11.If you want, choose a filter or edit the photo by adjusting brightness, contrast, or other settings.
12.Tap Next again.
13.Tap the Write a caption box.
14.Type a short message about the photo.
15.Add a location by tapping Add Location and selecting a place if needed .
16.Tag friends by tapping Tag People and selecting the people in the photo if needed.
17.Check that the correct photo, caption, and other details are shown.
18.Tap the Share button.
19.Wait a few seconds while Instagram uploads the photo.
20.When the upload is complete, the photo will appear on your Instagram profile and may also appear in your followers' feeds.
21.Stop.
